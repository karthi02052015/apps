SPY — Official Combat & Resource Rules

1. Weapons & Reload Mechanics

  • Single Gun Reload: Adds +50% charge (2 reloads = 100% ready to fire).
  • Double Gun Reload: Adds +100% charge (1 reload = 100% ready to fire).
  • Minigun Reload: Grants +4 Minigun bullets (1 shot = 1 bullet).

2. Shields, VP & Reflector Limits

  • Both Player and AI start at 50% VP (100% Full Gauge).
  • Using any standard Shield (Single, Double, Minigun) costs 25% VP (50% → 25% → 0%).
  • VP Restore: Usable whenever VP drops below 50% (at 25% or 0%). Restores VP back to 50% and grants a protective shield dome during that turn.
  • Blitz & Double Reflector: Maximum of 3 uses each per game.

3. Combat Resolution Matrix

  • Reload / VP Restore Immunity: Built-in protective shield! Cannot die if shot during that turn (0 pts; shooter spends ammo; reload/restore succeeds).
  • Gun vs. Gun: Tie (0 points, both spend ammo).
  • Gun vs. Matching Shield: Reflects bullet! Gun holder dies, Shield holder gets +1 Point.
  • Gun vs. Mismatched Shield: Shield fails! Shield holder dies, Gun holder gets +1 Point.
  • Blitz / Double Reflector vs. Any Gun: Reflects any gun! Gun holder dies, Reflector holder gets +1 Point.
  • Superior vs. Superior: Two Blitzes, two Double Reflectors, or Blitz vs. Double Reflector = Tie (both die, 0 points).
